Project info

⚡ Smart Meter

Kurdistan electricity calculator & guide — built by BHM Team, a team of three developers, to help citizens know their electricity cost quickly and easily.

6Subscriber types
5Home tariff tiers
3Languages
7Database tables

Features

Bill calculator

Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly cost using official tariffs — home (72–350 IQD per kWh), commercial, industrial, government and agricultural.

Amp → Watt converter

Convert amperes to watts at 220V, 180V or 110V and send the result straight to the calculator.

My Home

Add your home devices and see total monthly cost, kWh, and total amps against the 40A limit.

Bills & charts

Track monthly bills on a dynamic curve chart, and scan a paper bill with the camera (OCR).

Smart alerts

Get notified when usage passes 400, 800, 1200 or 1600 kWh, or when load reaches 40 amps.

Saving tips, history & FAQ

Practical tips to cut your bill, saved calculation history, and answers to common questions.

Personal settings

Kurdish, Arabic and English, dark/light mode, a custom main color, and export results as an image.

Accounts & security

User accounts with brute-force login protection and protected server configuration.
